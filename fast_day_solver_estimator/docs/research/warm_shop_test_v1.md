# New shop calendars for the complete compiler

Freeze both existing deferral policies before extracting twelve new shop/environment worlds. The primary candidate is unchanged .02 whole-call deferral; the secondary candidate is unchanged .10 cold-stage deferral after repair failure. Compare both against the original compiler under exactly the same shop fixture. The only compiler-source change is the fixture header include. The new header reads eight validated shop digits from `LABOR_WARM_SHOPS`; observations still reveal only the shop entries currently unlocked by the engine. Neither source nor rival receives the future sequence.

Eight worlds preserve the first three original shops and change the five later shops. Their fourth shop alternates smoothie and yarn, two shop types absent from the old fixture; subsequent entries and the new environment seeds are derived from fixed SHA strings in the preparer. Four more worlds use complete permutations of all eight shop types, also fixed by SHA order. No sequence or seed duplicates the old tests. These are designed transfer cases, not a random population estimate. Keep the source/rival agents and seven specifications fixed so that this test focuses on changed market and input calendars.

Apply the existing compiler preconditions at each specification's original expansion day: exactly three quadrants owned, intended expansion cells locked and a strict source witness. Record every excluded world/specification without substitution, along with all extracted daily contracts. Eligibility uses no model score or candidate outcome. Failure after eligibility, including a native backend crash, remains an outcome with its complete measured CPU. Do not silently stop the benchmark on such a child failure or rerun it.

Before opening these worlds, the adapter must reproduce the old fixture's source/rival action hashes and cash, all 90 saved source-day problem/action files, and two independent complete-course verification reports. Record the small source diff and all model/source/binary hashes. Keep running seed-test binaries unchanged.

Each eligible world/specification runs all three methods once. Assign all six within-case method permutations cyclically in SHA order for balanced ordering. Measure complete child CPU, including launcher, source construction, reuse, repair, cold fallback, in-compiler endpoint checks and artifact writes. Independently replay every completed course's 719 real transitions and match both players' cash and production; post-run verification is outside all timed invocations.

The primary gate needs at least 24 pairs across six worlds, at least 20% mean CPU saving, a strictly positive paired world-cluster 95% interval for saved CPU, no lost verified courses, no higher mean common-success hire bill, and equal common-success production. Apply the same gate to the predeclared secondary stage comparison and identify it as secondary. Report individual bill increases/decreases, native failures, uncompleted courses and shop-group/world/specification slices. Preserve the earlier fixed-fixture failures and passed new-seed result; this test cannot retroactively erase them.

The initial stage-seed driver stopped on a native solver assertion; a separate driver now completes only unstarted frozen calls and preserves that failure. The shop benchmark records native failures without ending the entire panel. This changes failure accounting in the driver, not the candidate compiler or backend behavior.
