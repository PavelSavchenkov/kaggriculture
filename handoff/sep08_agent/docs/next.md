## 1. General funding repair after a composition edit

Input: beginning state, proposed worker schedule, all inherited/new orders,
required pickup deadlines and fixed spending. Output: a funded order schedule
and replay certificate, or the earliest unsatisfied obligation. Optimize actual
whole-game value while preserving required physical production.

Witness: `runs/mixed_funding_sep08_001`. Day9 reduced early wheat quantities and
delayed two units. Day10 split a two-goose purchase into hours1/10 after extra
hires made the original quantity only partly affordable. All21 changed days and
30 endpoints now audit exactly. The repair is currently case-specific.

Test a bounded general repair that measures accepted quantities, splits or moves
unfunded obligations before pickup, replays joint market orders, then rechecks
endpoints against the reacting opponent. Do not blindly add a larger cash buffer.

## 2. Rival future-flow forecast and robust valuation
