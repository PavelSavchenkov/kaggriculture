# Fixed course with public-history timing

Experimental C++ agent. Default: Atakan course 0. The constructor also supports courses 1 and 2, and a control that disables timing while retaining purchase repair. At turn 226 it compiles its fixed course into a resource calendar using its own observation and a fixed sampled future weed seed. The calendar is an estimate, not a certified execution under real funding.

The timing rule delays at most one output sale by one turn across known demand. It checks public rival harvest/sale history and current ready yield, own projected storage and cash, and current commitments. Pending sales are inserted before new purchase repairs. Wheat and fertilizer are excluded from waiting. Future rival actions, private rival inventory and the actual environment seed never enter the policy. No claim of live-agent gain before the controlled tests.

External strategic lineage: the fixed farm courses come from the persistent `agents/external/atakan_demand/source/base` resource; see that agent's README for public provenance and rating scope. This wrapper adds the experiment's independently implemented finance components. It is not an independent farm strategy or a submitted agent. Preserve the original and repaired course controls.
