# Immediate premium sales

Test hypothesis derived from the September 9, 23:15 UTC top-player replay cohort. Otter Vibe and binghua sell nearly all observed premium inventory immediately, unlike the inherited parent. The observation is descriptive; the replay does not identify their private policy.

This C++ overlay changes sale quantities and appends sales for currently held carrot, tomato, strawberry, melon, egg, milk and wool. Wheat, fertilizer and purchase quantities remain under the existing parent policy. New sales use spare order positions in descending current unit price. The narrow variant acts only when inherited orders are sales or empty; `AllTurns` also acts on purchase turns.

Parent is this experiment's `room_keep`, ultimately persistent external `early_structure_cow`; the same provenance and reuse restrictions apply. No source policy copied from the discovered top players. Current observations only, separate instances, deterministic order. Full-game production/funding/league checks are required; more sales are not guaranteed to improve final cash.
