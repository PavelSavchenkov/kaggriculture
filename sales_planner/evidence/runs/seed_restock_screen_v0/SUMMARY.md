# Future-restock seed cap — rejected

The variant assumes supplied later seed-purchase requests can replace earlier stock. It fails the execution gate: after 78 of 394 planned cases, episode 107202365 seat 0 reaches turn 193 with six workers instead of the nine required by the source action. The batch stops and all 316 incomplete cases remain reported.

Among completed cases, three already change farming and reduce our cash by $1,878, $1,864 and $1,376. Their positive margins come from even greater rival losses and changed shop realizations. Those are not valid fixed-plan financial gains. Other cases change only interim seeds. Do not interpret the surviving mean as an all-case quality result.

Decision: reject this variant. A future purchase request is not proof of an available funded delivery. Preserve the global cap as a small unpromoted endpoint tool and keep the confirmed sale rules unchanged.
