# Measurement and promotion v0

Freeze each comparison's input families, candidate method, source hashes, budgets and metrics before its final run. Discovery may guide changes; final-test families are kept unexamined until freezing. Related replay/tape families and all their derived layouts stay together, even when published by different teams.

## Baselines

1. Original valid placement, with the same total compute spent on workforce/routes.
2. Current simple placement rules: source layout when available, owned/nearest-first and estimator-guided supplied candidates.
3. New placement search, using the same estimator and solver versions and comparable budget.

The method comparison must include user-requested rule-based, analytical/closed-form-based and heuristic-search families. Add further candidates from public patterns and session findings. Report quality versus speed curves and family-specific failures, not only the single best aggregate result.

Keep identity/no-search controls and unchanged input/output checks. Do not compare new placement plus a larger solver budget with a weaker original. UNKNOWN stays in coverage denominators. Source-workforce counts are feasible upper bills only where a witness exists, never exact optima.

## Correctness gate

All reported accepted schedules pass strict day replay and complete remaining-game verification. Planned lives, output dates/amounts, input needs, actual transactions, cash, capacity and final resources must agree with their contract. Existing entities do not move. No phantom final-hour work. Zero-budget valid incumbents remain valid. Invalid/stale cached witnesses are rejected.

## Quality gate

Report the best exact remaining hire bill at several common CPU budgets, complete-plan coverage, time to first certificate, per-family changes and worst cases. Compare bill differences on common-success pairs and count gained/lost complete plans separately. A candidate must not lose a valid supplied incumbent. Require positive family-cluster evidence of lower bills, or additional complete plans with noninferior common-success bills, before claiming improvement.

Provisional materiality target: at least 5% mean hire-cost reduction versus equal-budget fixed-layout search on common-success held-out plans, with positive family-cluster 95% interval and no coverage loss. This is a decision target, not a forecast. Also show median and results excluding the largest gain. A useful component with a different tradeoff can be retained under an explicitly scoped result, not called a gate pass.

## Speed gate

At least 20% lower complete search CPU with no lost certificates, no higher mean common-success bill and no new incumbent regression, or a quality improvement within the original total budget. Record p50/p95 wall time, CPU, maximum RSS, solver calls, cache hits and estimator overhead. Charge candidate generation, features, compilation, reuse/repair and required verification consistently. Pure inference time is supporting evidence.

Freeze budgets after measuring the original complete-course cost; do not select them after seeing which method wins. Use a deterministic query/node protocol alongside wall-budget tests. Account for bounded solver variability with paired repeats where needed.

## Pipeline and agent gate

Demonstrate a complete composition-search use case: new or improved legal placements lead to complete funded farm alternatives, better exact choices or lower end-to-end cost. A day proxy cannot pass this requirement.

For any deployable agent, follow prompts/local_agent.md: generic/pair parity, debug legality, separate instances, reset, self/PASS, observation isolation and deterministic budgets. Use both seats, live opponents and frozen seed sets. Separate independent-shop causal panels from native RNG outcomes. Report wins/ties/losses, own/rival cash, margin and tails. A recorded tape is conditional evidence only.

## Public patterns

Pull current leaderboard metadata, several recent complete games per development team, and relevant current public code/discussions. Ask: fixed or adaptive locations; persistent tile roles; repeated service groups; shop/opponent dependence. Replays show realized behavior; code or diverse conditional evidence is needed to establish the decision mechanism. Record URLs, versions, timestamps and hashes. Keep negative findings and duplicate-source families.

Review progress, formulation, resource use and priorities every 20 minutes from 00:10:28 UTC. Preserve failures, pending work and source identities. No auto-promotion or submission at the eight-hour deadline.

## Repair gate

Required by the user: occasional weed and insufficient-money rules. Freeze a fault corpus and unaffected controls. Compare unmodified execution, cheap rule repair and bounded exact repair. Require successful repair of the declared supported fault classes, complete downstream validity, zero new faults on unaffected controls, and explicit lost obligations outside scope. Report recovered final cash/output, p50/p95/max repair time, extra hires and additional solver calls. Injecting arbitrary favorable cash or deleting obligations is not repair.
# Counterfactual before conditional rules

For demand-aware swaps, donor combinations and quadrant rules, first measure whether the intervention has useful headroom with composition, quantities and dated obligations held fixed. Only after repeated exact gains should we learn which currently observable conditions select the intervention. Keep the best-of-candidates result labeled as hindsight headroom; it is not a deployable selector's score.
