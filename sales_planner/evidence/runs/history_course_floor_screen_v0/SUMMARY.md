Repeat the already used 128-seed, six-opponent, three-course test with an exact projected-market-inventory equality guard. This is a diagnostic repeat, not fresh confirmation.

Across 9,216 paired games against purchase repair, mean margin gain is $31.563 (95% seed interval $29.381–33.821), with 7,000 gains, zero losses and 2,216 unchanged. No changes to either player's checked physical state, production, terminal shed stock, discards, worker-days or faults; no history underestimates.

Comparison with frozen history timing changes exactly two games and removes exactly two decisions. Both are the traced eight-wool delay: original final gain -$175, guarded gain +$15, improvement $190. Every other action hash and economic result matches. This fixes the measured mechanism without a price threshold. It does not establish universal safety or an independently confirmed stronger mean.
