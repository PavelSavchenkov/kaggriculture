# Exact submitted policy

The submitted C++ type combines the accepted adaptive farm, two later animal
families, observed-market valuation, certified day programs, a specific weed
repair, sale anticipation, premium-order priority and a q24 opening. These are
layered components; it is not an online global day solver or a free-form farm
planner on every action.

## Inherited farm

The recoverable mainline starts with replay-derived complete farms and locally
recompiled labor schedules, then adds shop-conditioned herd and crop choices.
The later mainline includes a tomato-versus-productive-wheat rotation, a late
strawberry fertilizer decision, an opening quantity search, a day13
crop/goose/cow/sheep choice, and a sheep-heavy continuation inferred from public
shop and rival behavior. The parent also anticipates eligible output sales after
step216 and removes zero-quantity non-input sales to preserve useful order slots.

The principal borrowed materials are Justin's base farm, Mengfei's tomato
calendar, get some fries' productive crop calendars, Bohann's first two market
turns, Thomas Tschinkel's public-router families, and Ahmed's market execution
ideas. Source episode/seat/hash and exact local changes are retained in the
component ledger and each package's provenance directory.

## New animal-family choices

`runs/animal_group_policy_sep08_001/source/library.cpp` and `data.inc` contain
190 dated programs. The family selector considers eligible entry states at
day15 and day20. It can retain the inherited plan or choose one of the complete
alternatives in the family. This is broader than one species substitution, but
still restricted to compiled entry states and available library alternatives.

For each candidate it checks the entry state and enough money for fixed costs,
planned purchases and a1000 cash buffer. It evaluates32 plausible future shop
sequences conditional on shops already observed. It values changed own flows
and shared-price effects on the public rival model, subtracts the difference in
compiled fixed costs, and scores mean margin gain minus0.5 standard deviation.
Waiting has score0. There is no true future-shop lookup.

Day20 strawberry demand is already observed: weighted strawberry-consuming
shop demand at least4 selects the corresponding continuation. Cow-family
future weed labor is priced as an equal mixture of two solved cases. These
are explicit model assumptions, not complete forecasts of every future state.

## Execution and guards

Once an animal family activates, it stays committed to that farm. At each day
start it selects the closest relevant complete program and measures the guard.
Guard fields include farm layout, growth/service state, land, unit position,
seed and shed stocks. It does not abandon the added animals for the old farm
when a guard misses. Misses are recorded and investigated in whole games.

There is one independently certified alternative for the cow choice on day23
when the specific weed state matches. It restores six wheat in its repair
witness. This is a targeted repair, not general replanning after every weed.
The remaining broad-study misses were classified as inherited tile38 wheat
divergence or an unused empty-coop build blocked by a tile73 weed.

Schedules use the actual current number of workers and finalized action metadata.
Episode state belongs to each agent instance; program tables are immutable.
The online agent uses the ordinary observation interface only.

## Market layers

- The animal-course sale layer projects own shed stock after legal current
  pickups/drops, looks at the committed next-hour orders, advances eligible
  non-input product sales, and suppresses the duplicated next request. It does
  not lead across hour23 or beyond the final action.
- Zero-quantity carrot-through-wool sales are removed from its order list.
- Ahmed V24's premium-sale priority moves positive non-wheat/non-fertilizer
  sales ahead of other orders from step216. Relative order inside each group
  remains unchanged.
- The q24 opening changes only the first buy/sell wheat quantities to24. It
  meaningfully changes rival financing and routing, especially against Yusuke
  and King; it is not evidence of more agricultural output.

See the private copied source under the catalog agent for exact constants.
The forwarding catalog wrapper changes construction and namespaces, not these
decisions. `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` records every transformed source byte.

## Limits

The policy adapts to observed shops and public rival behavior, but it has a
finite repertoire. Its estimates do not fully model rival future growth and
price responses. Entry guards constrain activation. General repair, mixed-farm
construction, layout search and multi-stage family branching remain unfinished.
The q24 counter improves the selected league's win score while worsening King
margins. Read the complete per-opponent results before choosing a new parent.
