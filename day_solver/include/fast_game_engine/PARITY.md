# Parity validation

## Oracle

All truth is generated by the installed official interpreter, not by another
model of the rules.

```text
kaggle-environments 1.32.7
inspect.getsource SHA-256:
bc8a54879ef02c7ea64b8b333d6a976f0ea65c4949149d01f463f23bccee653e
```

Both exporters refuse to run if this version or digest changes. Trace validation
also rejects truth tagged with another upstream version or digest.

## Transition corpus

The checked corpus contains 56 episodes, 29,626 transitions, and 29,682 states.

### Curated episodes

Eight 719-transition episodes cover dense competitive strategies, the official
starter strategy, and a deterministic lifecycle strategy. Together they exercise
all 18 unit operations and all six market operations, crop production and decay,
all three animals, both structures, care/feed/fertilizer behavior, shed overflow
and insertion order, land, hires, town demand, shop duplicates, atomic planting,
and large action quantities.

### State-aware differential fuzzing

`differential_fuzz.py` creates another 48 deterministic episodes directly through
the official interpreter:

- 24 complete 720-state episodes with official defaults;
- 24 edge episodes varying episode length, starting money, market-order limit,
  turns per day, shed capacity, weed probability, all town intervals, and hire
  multiplier;
- legal state-aware actions mixed with illegal normalized actions, unknown items,
  nonexistent-hand actions, negative quantities, zero, 40,000, and signed 32-bit
  endpoints;
- simultaneous two-player market traffic, including 259,584 submitted fuzz
  orders and repeated lockstep contention.

Observed fuzz coverage includes every operation, every crop, every animal, empty,
locked, weed, coop, pasture and plant tiles, the eight-shop cap, and up to 29
simultaneous units. The generated traces are retained under `traces/fuzz` so the
ordinary C++ validator does not depend on rerunning Python.

For every state, the validator checks a canonical FNV-1a digest over:

- step, day, hour and completion status;
- ordered unlocked shops;
- every market inventory and price;
- both balances, unit counts and ordered positions;
- land, daily hire state, shed total and every shed item;
- all seed counts;
- every unit inventory in Python dictionary insertion order;
- kind and all lifecycle fields for all 200 tiles.

Money and market inventory are additionally compared directly, producing useful
diagnostics before the full-state hash is checked.

The complete corpus is run twice: once with `Action::finalize()` metadata and once
through the engine's metadata-free fallback.

## Independent numeric checks

`price-check` exhaustively compares the official and C++ default price functions
for all nine products and every integer inventory from -20,000 to 40,000. Both the
cached and uncached C++ paths match all 540,009 values.

`rng-check` compares raw IEEE-754 bits from `random.Random.random()` and sequential
`randrange` results across 127 keys. It covers zero, 32/64-bit boundaries and the
actual `(seed * 1_000_003) ^ day` key schedule. All 15,748 outputs match exactly.

## Structural coverage and sanitizers

The transition corpus produces the following GCC GCov result for `sim.hpp`:

```text
Lines executed: 98.38% of 616
Branches executed: 98.60% of 570
Taken at least once: 88.77% of 570
Calls executed: 96.73% of 153
```

Unexecuted production lines are the `log10` custom-market shape, impossible enum
defaults, fail-fast configuration guards, and defensive invariant cleanup. The
out-of-cache price path is exercised separately by the exhaustive price test.

All 56 traces pass a combined AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer
build. All C++ test and benchmark sources compile cleanly with:

```text
-Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Wshadow -Wconversion
```

## Reproduction

```bash
make -C fast_game_engine extended-check
```

This regenerates the differential corpus using the pinned official interpreter,
runs normal and metadata-free validation, exhaustively validates pricing and RNG,
then produces branch coverage. It is deliberately much heavier than `make check`.

## Boundary

Parity claims apply to the typed C++ action model and the documented supported
configuration: 10x10 boards, at most 16 submitted market slots, the official
default market parameters, and inventory values representable by the selected
count type. Malformed JSON coercion, arbitrary board geometry and sparse custom
`marketParams` curves are not silently approximated. `KAG_WIDE_COUNTS` is
available for nonstandard rich configurations.
