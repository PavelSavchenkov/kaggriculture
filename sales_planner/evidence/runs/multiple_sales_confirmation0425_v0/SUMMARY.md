# Final public temporal confirmation

The unchanged multiple-sale rule gains $269.50 mean margin over original orders on 68 player plans from 34 newly acquired episodes (95% episode interval $183.07–$371.75). There are 62 gains, no losses and six unchanged plans. All jobs complete, all checked noncash state matches, and no history underestimates or own cash prediction errors occur.

Against the unchanged one-edit delivery rule, the gain is $12.00 (95% episode interval $5.57–$20.22): 20 gains, no losses and 48 unchanged plans. Win utility is unchanged between these timing variants. The multiple-sale variant makes 745 edits; edit count is diagnostic, not the objective.

The binary and variants were frozen before extracting or inspecting these new actions. Purchases are supplied as accepted quantities and dates from each funded source schedule. Rival actions remain fixed in this public counterfactual test. This supports transfer to later recorded plans; it does not establish independent code families, a live purchase optimizer or better composition selection.

All 34 episodes are now exposed. The descriptive profile follows in `../top_patterns0425_v0`; no variant was tuned on this confirmation.
