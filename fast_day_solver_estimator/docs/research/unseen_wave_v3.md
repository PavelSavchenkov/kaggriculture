## Decisions and acceptance

Replicate the ordinary cold-search gate: at least 20% lower mean time to reference-best bill than original geometry with common nearby-workforce ordering; positive family-bootstrap lower bound on saved time; no worse 30-second certificate rate or mean verified bill. Include actual C++ scoring and decision costs. Report retrospective time-to-best separately from complete runtime and observable stopping.

For marginal additions, require at least 20% lower family-macro cash MAE than original flat10, a positive family-bootstrap interval for error reduction, and no increase in the fraction of underestimates of at least 500. Evaluate small and large additions separately. Failure on either is not hidden by success on removals.

For explicit calendars, require at least 20% lower family-macro worker MAE than geometry with the same necessary floor, a positive family-bootstrap interval, and no increase in underestimates of at least two workers. Report H23 and H24 separately even if the pooled gate passes. Sparse labeled slices remain uncertain.

Difficulty flags are peak success below 0.5, probability at the direct point below 0.2, and their union. Report failure/error capture and the flagged fraction over all inputs including unknowns. A promising deferral screen should capture at least 80% of labeled underestimates of at least 500 while flagging at most 30%; confidence and absolute counts must accompany this development-selected threshold. No failed query is permanently discarded by a learned screen.

Observable refinement is secondary: compare exhaustive around search, stop at first certificate, one/two/four additional calls, three/ten-second refinement caps, and query-utility thresholds 0.1 and 1 cash per nominal CPU second. Keep original direct ranking before the first certificate. Measure complete CPU, certificate rate and verified-bill regret. Any bill-for-time tradeoff is reported explicitly; it does not establish a no-quality-loss speedup.
