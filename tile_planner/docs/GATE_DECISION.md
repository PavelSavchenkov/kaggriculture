# Decision against the original gates

This records the outcome against [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md). It does not revise the thresholds after seeing results.

| Gate | Decision | Evidence and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Accepted-course correctness | Passed on the reported accepted courses | Strict day replay, full engine execution and an independent financial calendar. Bounded searches retain a complete incumbent; failed prefixes are excluded. This is measured correctness, not a proof for arbitrary inputs. |
| General placement quality | Not passed | Six supported frozen final inputs have identical search/fixed outcomes at 180 seconds. The separate two-input distinct-family extension loses to fixed layout twice. Neither a 5% mean hire reduction nor positive independent-family evidence is established. |
| Broad end-to-end speed target | Not established | The real exact-reuse panel saves 19.2% of compile-plus-check wall time. Its 23.1% CPU saving measures compilation only, excluding the checker. Cached estimate and bank-loading gains have narrower scopes. No claim of 20% lower complete search CPU follows. |
| Scoped exact reuse | Retain | Packed banks, unchanged-day estimate caches and identical-lifetime compilation reuse preserve all checked scores/actions/outcomes. The clean package builds and its complete example verifies. Charge bank construction separately and replay every reused schedule. |
| Whole composition pipeline use | Demonstrated, with limited quality gains | New compositions become legal dated lives, daily work, complete executable courses and delivered/discarded calendars. Some synthetic placement changes improve complete outcomes. Real fixed-layout controls show why the new search should remain optional. |
| General weed/cash repair | Not passed | Weed recovery coverage improves, but some final-cash losses remain. Cash rules still have severe downstream losses. Unaffected controls pass; that alone is insufficient for promotion. |
| Deployable agent | Not evaluated as a new agent | This is an offline component and narrow conditional-placement demonstration. No new agent was submitted or promoted. |

The primary frozen method supports six of eight selected biological inputs. The repeated-service extension later supports the other two biologically; five of its eight compilation contexts complete. It does not retroactively change frozen coverage. The within-day overripe-decay development case remains unsupported.

The final teams are not four independent strategy families. The public-tape audit finds heavy sharing in three teams and a distinct pair from Unknown Mother-Goose. Reporting a family-cluster confidence interval from four independent teams would be unjustified. The all-tie primary result already fails the materiality target without that interval.

Search seeds, candidates, binaries, query outcomes and wall budgets are recorded. A deterministic node-budget reproduction of the complete placement search was not implemented. Solver time limits can alter search allocation; the later thirty-second point diagnostic is separate evidence, not a deterministic extension of the earlier run.

Retain the exact component in this experiment. Persistent-package integration would need its own interface review and callers' representative input tests. Current code and the source archive deliberately preserve the failed and unsupported boundaries described in [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md).
