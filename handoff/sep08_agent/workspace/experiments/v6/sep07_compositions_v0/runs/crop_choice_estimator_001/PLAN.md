# Observed-state crop choice

Compare complete wheat and tomato continuations at day 12 using only the real
observation. The current crop_mix_t2_wheat policy uses an empirical two-shop
tomato threshold. Keep it as the exact reference while testing whether cheap
dated economics can choose better across observed shops and opponents.

The own plans come from frozen complete wheat and tomato courses, with separate
day-20 berry continuations. Their intended quantities and fixed costs are legal
policy parameters. Future market quotes and real future shops are not inputs.
At each sampled future shop sequence, use the existing berry rule to select the
appropriate suffix. Keep the original physical entry guard and fallback.

Reuse the observation-only current-crop forecast and whole-portfolio market
valuation from public_crop_forecast_001, with exact attribution. Its prior audit
found that currently visible crops explained only 20.2% of later wheat and 5.7%
of later carrot harvests. Test explicit continuation assumptions: no crop
forecast, current crops only, repeated productive wheat/carrot cycles, and a
terminal carrot continuation after wheat when enough time remains. These are
public-state hypotheses learned from replay patterns, not recovered private
opponent code. Keep feed netting and animal-fertilizer supply separate controls.

First extract and verify the own complete-course financial plans. Use observed
day-12 market inventory and money; sample unknown shops with the existing
stratified 64-sequence method. Score both own profit and relative margin, and
retain model uncertainty about future planting, opponent purchases, service,
deposit timing, funding, storage and exact order interleaving. The initial
valuation uses daily midpoint quotes and is explicitly heuristic.

Use discovery seeds 1000 onward to compare estimated and exact alternative
outcomes on the same prefixes. Report branch regret, ranking, own and rival
product-level errors, actual crop output, costs, discarded stock and route
failures. A better quantity forecast is not sufficient for promotion. Only a
fixed executable policy with unused 1740000+ evidence, broad league and native
checks, complete component parity and operational validation may replace the
current reference. Keep final 900000 unused.

This run is a valuation and policy-choice experiment over two complete crop
families. It does not replace ongoing placement search, cold construction,
general animal selection including wait, or larger farm rebuilding.
