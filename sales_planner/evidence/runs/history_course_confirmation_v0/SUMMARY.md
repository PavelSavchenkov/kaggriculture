# Fixed courses against live opponents: history_course_confirmation_v0

Compare history-aware one-turn waiting with the same fixed course and purchase repair alone. Three Atakan courses, six exposed opponents, both seats and independent/native shop streams. The candidate uses legal public history and an own compiled resource calendar; rival actions are chosen live. Future weeds in compilation are sampled with a fixed seed, not the actual environment seed.

9,216 paired games from 128 environment seeds; all jobs completed. Mean final cash-margin gain $31.52, seed-cluster 95% interval $29.33 to $33.79. Own-cash gain is the same. 6,998 gains, 2 losses, 2,216 unchanged; worst $-175, best $230.

No changed own or rival physical state, production, terminal shed stock, discards or worker-days, and no added faults. No public-stock-bound underestimates. All calendars compiled. These exact checks concern the tested games; they do not certify every future scenario.

Mean complete own decision time per game, including observation construction and compilation: control 4.339 ms, candidate 4.751 ms. Candidate compilation accounts for 3.541 ms. Added mean time 0.412 ms. This is measured under the recorded concurrent batch load, not an isolated microbenchmark.

Win/tie utility changes by 0.0434 percentage points, interval 0.0000 to 0.1085. Mean margin is the clearer result. Worst-decile margin changes from $-30870.86 to $-30850.83; those are tails of the actual margin distributions, not the worst individual deltas.
