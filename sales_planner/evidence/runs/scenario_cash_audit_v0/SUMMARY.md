# Historical rival cash: exposed-cohort audit

This audit identifies a scenario-construction defect; its initial positive selector result is not a confirmed improvement.

Across 128 previously exposed seeds, five opponent packages and both seats, preserving historical rival cash removes all 2,019 failed rival branch forecasts. All 3,840 actual fixed-branch outcomes match. Mean chosen margin rises $540.18 against the old cash-splice selector (95% seed interval $56.16–$1,050.40), with 82 gains and 62 losses; worst loss -$16,115. Own cash falls $281.25 on average and rival cash falls $821.44. Superiority over the older model chooser is unproved in this cohort.

All 144 changed choices are cases where the original evaluator rejected every branch and used its demand fallback. Numeric best-branch rankings change in zero of 1,280 decisions. The observed gain comes from changed eligibility, not improved relative price prediction.

The unchanged fresh 12-opponent test in `../scenario_cash_confirmation_v0` does not confirm a reliable gain over the old selector or demand chooser. Do not promote from this exposed cohort. Borrowed farm calendars also need public-state compatibility before being described as forecasts of the observed rival; see `../scenario_public_compatibility_v0`.

Exact comparisons, all selected losses and attribution are retained in `historical_cash/PAIRED_EFFECTS.json`, `SELECTION_AUDIT.json` and `RANK_ATTRIBUTION.json`. The original failed forecasts remain recorded.
