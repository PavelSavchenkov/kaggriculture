# Rival earliest sale bound

166 exposed episodes, both seats, 1,670,956 product observations and 47,989 actual sale events. Zero sales earlier than any previously stated bound; zero held-stock underestimates. 97,312 observations with ready rival output admit at least one extra waiting turn. Mean bound cost is 0.248 microseconds per observation; complete batch 2.04 seconds.

Bound = minimum of existing worker travel + harvest/deposit time, optimistic newly hired worker route, and next night. Possible private stock gives no guaranteed waiting time. This is a correctness/opportunity audit, not a policy-quality result. See PROTOCOL.json and all per-case outputs.
