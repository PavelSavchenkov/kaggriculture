surveyed in 2,048 complete games against eight opponents; 64 standard-runner
controls match complete action hashes and final cash. The cow entry matches
223 games; the sheep entry matches 37. These are eligibility counts, not wins.

both players' final cash and every daily guard match the independent audits.

STUDY_RESULTS.md/json contains 8,960 discovery games and 1,152 exact full-record
controls against the previous arena. Economic selectors activate in 68/1,280
games each. Neutral-opponent wins are unchanged; mean margin gains are $174.66
with fixed market orders and $197.93 with live sale anticipation. Always adding
cows regresses neutral win utility. No policy is promoted from this evidence.

on day23. Its newly added cows still produce as intended, but a wheat planting
